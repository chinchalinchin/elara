--------
Jasilynn
--------

- June - July 2026

Sonnet #1
^^^^^^^^^

.. verse::
  :increment: 5

  this heart was made to pump my blood around,
  to feel the sun on fingertips inside
  the chambers where the currents run aground
  and darkness leaves the warmth of it implied.

  commotion sweeps away what parts collide,
  as skin begins to stick to her again
  and thoughts recede upon the breaking tide,
  the gentle lips of sighing Jasilynn.

  the ocean claims the lives of countless men,
  their water graves become the pulse of veins;
  the drying words of ink within my pen
  are made of what their ancient blood contains.

  her eyes awash in mine, we drift and sway
  to let the waves convey our hearts away.

Sonnet #2
^^^^^^^^^

.. verse::
  :increment: 5

  my ears are deaf to music in your mind,
  though eyes betray the notes it softly plays,
  in ways your stare invades with aims designed
  to keep my own with yours in steady gaze.

  mistakes are made because my fingers graze
  the pipes that gently shape your tongue to speak,
  and feel the sounds before they breach airways,
  the tremors building up from cheek to cheek.
  
  what words escape are afterthoughts that leak
  despite restraint you place upon their choice;
  what tears then follow after only seek
  the force contained within your inner voice.

  the things unsaid then linger there awhile
  as lips that tremble try their best to smile.

Sonnet #3
^^^^^^^^^

.. verse::
  :increment: 5

  oh Jasilynn, what rib divided us apart?
  what force from when the universe began
  decreed our ending from the very start?
  what manner god conceives this kind of plan?

  what hand has made within my chest a span
  to compass round the empty realm it keeps?
  what type of coward makes a broken man
  who sits in silence as his lover weeps?

  within the silence, longing never sleeps,
  yet always lengths untrodden stretch ahead,
  as though, from other worlds, a distance sweeps 
  and stops my feet approaching where you tread.

  my heart was never made to hold the weight
  of things in expectation you await.

Sonnet #4
^^^^^^^^^

.. verse::
  :increment: 5

  my every thought is made to gaze on you,
  though eyes imagine more than what remains,
  despite your absence, as the days accrue,
  of moments now distilled within my veins. 

  the line you traced upon my skin retains
  electric residues of fingertips
  whose glow for now provides but ever wanes,
  as current seeps discharged in leaks and drips.

  my heart begins its nightly new eclipse,
  as dwarfed within the shadow of your own,
  and sunk below horizons where it dips
  in darkness, there to fester all alone.

  a lack confirms my heart was shaped for you,
  yet known too late this simple thing was true.
